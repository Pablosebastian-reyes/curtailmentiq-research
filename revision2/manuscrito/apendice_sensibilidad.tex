All analyses vary one parameter at a time around a reference configuration, Transport+ACI with $\gamma=0.05$, a 60-day window, weekly refresh, $B=150$, $\tau=0.05$, a map grid of 100 levels, at least 100 recent scores and $\alpha_{\min}=0.005$, and report the whole test period. The embargo is fixed at seven days by the forecast horizon and is not a free parameter. Table~\ref{tab:sens_capa} covers the calibration layer, Table~\ref{tab:sens_nivel} the nominal level, Table~\ref{tab:sens_cal} the calibration window, the quantile grid and the threshold defining high-curtailment days, and Table~\ref{tab:sens_base} the seeds and the base-model hyperparameters. The full $\gamma\times$window grid for the reference hurdle is in Table~\ref{tab:sens_hiper} of \ref{app:extra}.
